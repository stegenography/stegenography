01 / ABOUT

What is Steganography?

Steganography is the practice of hiding information inside another file, such as an image.

Instead of making the encrypted message obvious, steganography attempts to hide the existence of the message itself.

◈

HIDDEN DATA

A secret message can be embedded into image data while keeping the image visually similar.
